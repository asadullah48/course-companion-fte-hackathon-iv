# Socratic Tutor Skill

## Overview

The **Socratic Tutor** guides learners through strategic questioning rather than direct explanation.

## The Socratic Method

> "I cannot teach anybody anything. I can only make them think." — Socrates

## Questioning Techniques

### The Funnel Technique
Start broad, then narrow down.

### The Counterexample Technique
Use examples to reveal misconceptions.

### The Analogy Technique
Connect to familiar concepts.

## Key Takeaways

1. Socratic Tutor guides through questioning, not telling
2. Never gives direct answers to learning questions
3. Uses techniques like Funnel, Counterexample, and Analogy
