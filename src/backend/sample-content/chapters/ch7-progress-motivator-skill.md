# Progress Motivator Skill

## Overview

The **Progress Motivator** keeps learners engaged through achievement tracking, streak management, and personalized encouragement.

## Achievement System

Achievements gamify the learning experience:
- 🏆 First Steps - Complete your first chapter
- 🔥 Week Warrior - Maintain a 7-day streak
- 📚 Knowledge Seeker - Complete all chapters in Module 1
- ⚡ Speed Learner - Complete a chapter in under 10 minutes

## Streak System

Streaks encourage daily learning habits:
- Track consecutive days of learning
- Provide encouragement messages
- Offer streak freezes for recovery

## Key Takeaways

1. Progress Motivator tracks completions, streaks, and achievements
2. Gamification elements increase engagement
3. Personalized encouragement maintains motivation
