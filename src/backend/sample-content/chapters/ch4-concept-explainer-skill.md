# Concept Explainer Skill

## Overview

The **Concept Explainer** is one of the four core AI skills in the Course Companion system.

## The Role of Concept Explainer

- **Breaks down complex topics** into understandable pieces
- **Adapts to learner level** (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- **Connects to prior knowledge** using course content
- **Provides examples and analogies** for better understanding

## Explanation Patterns

### The "What-Why-How" Pattern
1. **What**: Define the concept clearly
2. **Why**: Explain why it matters
3. **How**: Show how it works in practice

### The "Analogy Bridge" Pattern
Connect new concepts to familiar ones.

### The "Layered Disclosure" Pattern
Reveal complexity gradually.

## Key Takeaways

1. Concept Explainer adapts to learner level and context
2. Uses MCP to retrieve relevant course content
3. Follows structured patterns for explanations
